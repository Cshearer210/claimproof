# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue with the code, not the person.

## What is expected

- **Assume competence.** Someone reporting a false positive has read your gate
  more carefully than most. Treat the report as the gift it is.
- **Criticise the work, never the author.** "This rule flags correct code and
  here is the case" is review. "This is sloppy" is not.
- **Show your evidence.** This project asks agents to back their claims; the
  same standard applies to people making assertions in issues and reviews.
- **Accept a no.** Not every idea belongs here, and scope is stated in
  CONTRIBUTING.md. A declined change is not a judgement of you.

## What is not tolerated

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about anyone's identity or
experience level, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption. Nobody
needs a longer list than that to know which side of the line they are on.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to cshearer210@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept private. Responses range from a private word to removing comments to
blocking an account, chosen to fit what happened.

Enforcement is by a human using judgement, not by rule-lawyering. If your
defence is a close reading of this document's wording, you have already lost
the thread of it.
